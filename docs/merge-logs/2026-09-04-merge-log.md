## Corrections to earlier diagnoses in this pass

Both of my initial root-cause calls were wrong and were corrected from evidence:

- **#463's `test-integration` hang** was not the `_pending` strong-reference set. Pristine `main`
  plus migration `035` alone hangs identically. The cause is a pre-existing `reset_db()` pool leak:
  a fire-and-forget audit INSERT whose event loop closed leaves a backend in `ClientRead` holding
  `RowExclusiveLock` on `audit_log`, blocking the next startup's migration `008`. Restoring the
  audit trail is what made the leak *reachable* — before `035`, every insert failed fast and
  released.
- **#464's red `test-integration`** was not issue #473 alone. #464's own narrowed allowlist stopped
  the first-run migration pass at `019`, after `015` had re-run and clobbered `025`'s trigger
  rewrite. #473 downgraded from "breaks integration tests" to latent.
