### Fixes

**Consensus synthesizer** (`157d7bc1`, issue #358 filed and closed). The zero-match behavior was structural, not data-dependent — three defects in `match_score()` each independently sufficient to zero most cross-vendor pairs:

1. Both reachable score bands required byte-identical `file_path` strings; vendors emit the same file as repo-relative, absolute, and `a/`-diff-prefixed paths.
2. A hard equality gate on free-form `type` labels sat in front of every band ("correctness" vs "bug" → 0.0, even at the same line).
3. The no-file `type+description` band scored `min(0.3 + jaccard*0.3, 0.7)` — clearing the 0.6 threshold required Jaccard 1.0, i.e. verbatim-identical prose from independent LLMs. Dead code.
